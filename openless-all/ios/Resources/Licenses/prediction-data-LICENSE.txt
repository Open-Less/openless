OpenLess modifications:
- Reproducible selection and SQLite conversion by scripts/build-predictions.py.
- ASCII English tokens are retained; up to 32 continuations per context.
- Chinese phrase continuations contain up to 8 suffixes per context.
- These are statistical dictionaries, not Apple's QuickType language model.
- The bundled database is read-only. Queries stay on-device and do not store
  the user's document text, typed words, or input history.

Exact source URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in Prediction/manifest.json.
