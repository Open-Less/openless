Generation: this repo's scripts/generate-english-dictionary.mjs filters the
source list to plain lowercase English words (rejecting subtitle-tokenizer
contraction fragments such as "'s"/"n't", numbers, and non-letter tokens),
keeps the top 20,000 by frequency, and writes them as tab-separated
"word<TAB>count" rows sorted by descending frequency. No runtime code in
OpenLess downloads, modifies, or redistributes the original source files —
only this generated, filtered derivative ships in the app.
